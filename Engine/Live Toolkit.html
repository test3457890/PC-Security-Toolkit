🛡️ iPad & iPhone Security Checklist

        
Passcode + Face ID / Touch ID: Settings > Face ID & Passcode. Use 6 digits or more. This is also what encrypts the device.

        
Stolen Device Protection: Settings > Face ID & Passcode > turn ON. Stops a thief who saw your passcode from changing your Apple Account.

        
Find My iPad / iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Find My > ON, plus "Send Last Location".

        
Automatic updates: Settings > General > Software Update > Automatic Updates > turn on Security Responses and iPadOS/iOS updates.

        
App tracking & Safari privacy: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > turn OFF "Allow Apps to Request to Track". In Safari turn on "Hide IP Address".

        
Lockdown Mode (optional): Settings > Privacy & Security > Lockdown Mode. Extreme protection for travel or if you think you're being targeted.

        
Android: Private DNS: Settings > Network > Private DNS > dns.quad9.net blocks malware and phishing sites.

        
Android: turn USB debugging OFF in Developer Options when you are done scanning.

        
Kids' iPad? See the Screen Time steps in the 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Plan tab.
